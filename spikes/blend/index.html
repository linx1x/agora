The two formulas

Ground
  score_t = w_t + 0.5·(noise_t − ½) + 0.28·(height_t − ½)
  w_t: weight of terrain t over the 4 nearest cell centres
  pixel = texture of the best score, sampled in world space

Objects
  d = min over the boxes of every joined cell
  top face: d < 0 · front face: top swept down 10 px
  light = dot(∇d, top-left) near the edge
  outline on the outer edge only, seam where material changes
  level → jungle ramp through a 4×4 Bayer threshold

What to look at

  • The three desks running into two counters: one top, one outline, a seam only where the material changes.
  • The L and T: one continuous top, planks turning with the arm, legs only at the real ends.
  • Lamps resting on the desk with a contact shadow and a warm pool of light.
  • The dirt trail against the hand-drawn path tiles on the left.
