Agora 2.5D benchmark

The Jungle wing drawn in a tilted 3D view, built twice: once with Three.js and once with Phaser 4 projecting the same faces itself. Run it here on your laptop and on your phone. Each result is saved below, so Claude can read the numbers for Checkpoint I.

Run on this device

  1. Plug in the laptop and close other heavy tabs. On a phone, keep the screen awake.
  2. The full run plays six scripted walks of 15 seconds each (about 2 minutes). Don't touch the page while it runs. On a phone, a quick run of each engine is enough.

    Three.js

    A real 3D engine: one merged mesh for the wing, real-time sun and lamp lights, optional sun shadows. It adds about 130 KB (gzipped) to the app.

    Phaser 4, faked depth

    The app's current engine. Phaser 4 has no 3D camera, so every face is projected on the CPU each frame. No dynamic lights. Adds nothing to the app.

    Walking: arrow keys or WASD, or click or tap the floor. The panel in the corner changes the tilt, the distance, the number of bots and the pixel filter.
